GOOD DATA / FOUR RULES

We'd rather miss a count than invent certainty.

  1. No listing without an organiser link.
  2. Past events disappear from the upcoming feed.
  3. The same event from FMAA and UNSW appears only once.
  4. Restricted eligibility and unknown times stay visible.

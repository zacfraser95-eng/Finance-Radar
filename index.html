GOOD DATA / FOUR RULES

We'd rather miss a count than invent certainty.

  1. No listing without an organiser link.
  2. Past events disappear from the upcoming feed.
  3. The same event from FMAA and UNSW appears only once.
  4. Restricted eligibility and unknown times stay visible.
NEXT SOURCE TO UNLOCK

Newsletters & society partnerships.

FINSOC and other societies may advertise only through private social channels. A forwarded newsletter inbox or an approved organiser event feed is the most robust fallback. There is no reliable way to promise complete coverage from public scraping alone.

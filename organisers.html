Finance Radar is an independent, free events guide for UNSW and Sydney students. If your society runs a finance panel, workshop, careers night or networking session, help us share the public details in one place.

01

Send the official listing

Submit the event title, date, start time, location, registration URL, cost and student eligibility. Use a public organiser page or event-registration link, not a private screenshot. The submission form uses GitHub and requires a free GitHub account.

02

We verify before publishing

A submission does not go straight onto the website. We check the public source and dates first. If details are incomplete, the event stays unpublished or is clearly marked for review.

03

Share an ongoing events feed

If your society has a public events page, RSS/ICS calendar or registration hub, include it in the submission. We can consider adding it to our regular checks, with your society credited and each event linking to the official registration.

For transparencyFinance Radar is an independent student project, not an official UNSW or society website. We don't sell places or take registration payments. Only publicly available event information is accepted; societies retain control of their registrations and event details. We don't need passwords, private group access or mailing-list member data.
